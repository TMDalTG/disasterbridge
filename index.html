🌐 DisasterBridge

AI-powered emergency response for individuals with disabilities during natural disasters — from risk mapping to welfare calls to first responder briefings.

⚡ Powered by Groq + Llama 3.1 ✓ Open Source ❤ Built for humanitarian impact
⚠️

During natural disasters, people with disabilities face two layers of risk: immediate physical danger from the disaster itself, and loss of access to the support systems and equipment they depend on daily. DisasterBridge uses AI to close that gap — identifying who needs help, reaching them proactively, and getting the right information to first responders instantly.

Tools

🗺️
Risk Map
Hurricane Beryl scenario — animated vulnerability mapping across Houston. Watch as critical residents are flagged and resources deployed in real time. Automatically saves flagged residents to the attention dashboard.
✓ No API key needed
📞
Welfare Call Simulator
AI dispatcher places welfare check calls to residents, adapting tone and pacing per disability profile. Auto mode or interactive — you speak as the resident. Cards are built and saved live from each call.
🔑 Free Groq key needed
🚨
People Requiring Attention
Central dashboard of everyone flagged by the risk map or welfare calls. View, print, and update first responder briefing cards. Cards merge and improve with each new call or manual update.
⟳ Auto-populated from calls & map

Getting started

1

Get a free Groq API key at console.groq.com/keys — sign up with any email, click Create API key. No credit card needed.

2

Open the Risk Map and click Run Simulation — residents are automatically added to the People Requiring Attention dashboard as they are flagged.

3

Open Welfare Calls, paste your key in the yellow bar, and place a call. The responder card updates live and saves to the dashboard automatically.

4

Open People Requiring Attention to see everyone flagged, view or print their cards, and update details as the situation changes.
